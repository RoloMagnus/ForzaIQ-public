Overall evaluation methodology

In possession · Build Up, Create and Finishing
Build Up

Beat the first line of pressure. Use the goalkeeper and support angles. Find the free player to progress.

Create

Open a route to goal. Receive between lines or switch play. Combine to break the last line.

Finishing

Turn an opening into a goal. Time runs and occupy the box. Choose the final pass or shot.

Out of possession · High Block, Middle Block and Low Block
High Block

Disrupt the opponent's build up. Press together near their goal. Protect the space behind.

Middle Block

Deny central progression. Stay compact around midfield. Press when a trap or trigger appears.

Low Block

Protect the goal and penalty area. Close gaps and defend the box. Keep an outlet for the regain.

Load and injury-risk monitoring

Repeated high-speed bursts forward and long returns can stack up load, so the aim is to remove low-value sprints, not to cut the useful ones. Request GPS high-speed running metres (with the velocity cut-off), maximum speed, accelerations and decelerations, session-RPE and wellness; split sprints by purpose (attacking run, press, recovery) using the video; review congested periods. Club S&C and medical staff sign off every change. No injury prediction is made from sprint counts, and no acute:chronic cut-off is applied.

DEFINITIONSMethod and formulas

Definitions and formulas

  • Per 90 = stat × 90 / minutes (recomputed from minute-summed totals; never averaged across matches).
  • Gap = player − peer median; % difference = gap / median × 100.
  • Radar index = value / reference maximum × 100. Rank position = (N − rank) / (N − 1) × 100.

  • Sprints, HSR, intensive runs: event counts per 90 above undocumented thresholds, not distances.
