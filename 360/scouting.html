ASSIGNMENTBrief: objective and deliverable rules

Recruitment Recommendation brief

Objective

Act as a club's Recruitment Analyst: compare transfer targets using their profiles, statistics and match footage, and conclude with one signing recommendation that is justified by objective evidence and a clearly separated subjective evaluation. The assignment compares two targets; this extended version compares three.

Deliverable rules

  • A concise, easy-to-scan PDF dossier plus a recorded explanation (3–5 minutes); both must support the same signing decision.
  • Integrate age, market value and injury history with performance data.
  • Distinguish objective evidence from interpretation; explain where video and data agree or differ.
  • Acknowledge missing information; do not invent it. Market value is an estimate, not a confirmed transfer fee.

Price against precedent

Reading the fee ranges

  • Mehmeti: the modelled range sits around Frankfurt's biggest recent midfield buy (Uzun, €13.0m); the median is below it, and his contract shrinks to 18 months by Jul 2027.
  • Lira: the median (€17.8m) is above Uzun and the top of the range (€27.3m) is far outside precedent; his value is at a peak.
  • Bangoura: a fraction of the cost (€0.9–1.8m); the risk is the sample, not the price.

Definitions and data limits

  • Per 90 = Driblab total / (minutes / 90).
  • Position between worst and best of five = (value − worst) / (best − worst) × 100, inverted for fouls and turnovers.
  • xT (expected threat) and xA (expected assists) are Driblab metrics; pass completion over expected = pass % minus expected pass % (percentage points).
  • Fit rating: Strong 3, Good 2, Partial 1, Weak 0, equal weights across 7 criteria.
  • Driblab has no league adjustment; its match dates, competition and season are not in the CSVs, and sample lengths differ (757 to 2,876 minutes).
  • Players are often used in several positions; multiple role tags are normal.
  • Driblab's turnover counts are very small; read them as dangerous losses only.
  • Market value is a crowd-sourced estimate, not a fee. Ranks are reliable, decimals are not.
