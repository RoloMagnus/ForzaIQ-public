Overall evaluation methodology

In possession · Build Up, Create and Finishing
Build Up

Beat the first line of pressure. Use the goalkeeper and support angles. Find the free player to progress.

Create

Open a route to goal. Receive between lines or switch play. Combine to break the last line.

Finishing

Turn an opening into a goal. Time runs and occupy the box. Choose the final pass or shot.

Out of possession · High Block, Middle Block and Low Block
High Block

Disrupt the opponent's build up. Press together near their goal. Protect the space behind.

Middle Block

Deny central progression. Stay compact around midfield. Press when a trap or trigger appears.

Low Block

Protect the goal and penalty area. Close gaps and defend the box. Keep an outlet for the regain.

Priority 1 · In possession / Finishing

Shot selection and box arrival

DATA Shots 1.88/90 and xG 0.23/90 (42nd of 46 strikers); 0.124 xG per shot; 7 goals from 6.45 xG.

VIDEO 44:56 and 64:03: well-intended shots that miss the target; body position and finishing control in congested areas.

DO
  • Finishing blocks from wide-right and half-space entry points, with the cue to shoot or cut back arriving late.
  • Box-arrival runs timed to the far-post and cutback zones, with a shot or a pass decision on each repetition.

TRACK Shots and xG per 90; xG per shot; share of shots from central zones inside the box.

Priority 2 · In possession / Create

Turn creation into assists

DATA 38 key passes and 10 big chances created for 1 assist; cross success 27.1% on 2.3/90; 1v1 success 42.9% on a tiny sample.

VIDEO 3:20 and 67:30: a long cross or long ball chosen over a better option; 62:32 is logged without a coaching point.

DO
  • 1v1-to-final-ball patterns: take on, then pick the cutback, the low cross or the recycle, with a coach-set rule for each zone.
  • Crossing blocks aimed at the penalty spot and the far post from the byline and from deeper channels.

TRACK Cross success; key passes and big chances created per 90; assists against big chances created.

Priority 3 · Transitions / Out of possession

Positioning and anticipation out of possession

DATA Sprints 14.9/90 and HSR 36.8/90 (CSV, no units); 38 tackles and 51 fouls committed over the season.

VIDEO 1:06, 28:56, 26:02, 32:04, 82:15: late cover of the winger's space, not pinning #20, waiting for the ball, losing the blind side, and an unclosed diagonal.

DO

  • Receive-and-protect habit: run toward the ball, or shield it with the body, before the defender arrives; scan before every restart.
  • Diagonal pressing runs that cut the passing lane and the space together.

DEFINITIONSMethod, formulas and sources

Definitions and formulas

  • Per 90 = stat × 90 / minutes (recomputed from totals, never averaged across matches).
  • Gap = player − peer median; % difference = gap / median × 100.
  • Radar index = value / reference maximum × 100. Rank position = (N − rank) / (N − 1) × 100.
  • xG per shot = xG / shots. Goals − xG is an outcome gap, not proof of finishing skill.
  • Sprints, HSR, intensive runs: event counts per 90 above undocumented thresholds, not distances. Distance units are unspecified.
  • Chances created (Statz) = key passes; big chances created is a separate, stricter provider event.
  • Cross, 1v1 and aerial % = successes / attempts; tiny denominators make 0% and 100% misleading.
  • Blank = not visible in source, not zero.
